To fix the "too much text" problem: Tell Replit to build a "Card-Based UI". Instead of text descriptions for apartments, the feed should look like Instagram or TikTok. Big image of the room, a bold price (e.g., $600/mo), and three icons underneath (🛏️ 1 Bed, 🛁 1 Bath, 📍 Green St). That’s it.

🕵️‍♂️ 2. How Does the App "Know" a Sublease is Successful?
Because the actual lease signing happens offline at a UIUC property management office (like JSM or Smile), the app cannot automatically know. You have to force the users to tell the app.

How? By using incentives and annoyance:

The "Stop the Spam" Incentive: When a user's sublease is popular, their inbox will blow up. Put a massive, satisfying #E75B64 (Ponyo Red) button on their dashboard that says "Mark as Leased". They will click this just to stop getting messages from random people.

The Trigger: When they click that button, the app immediately removes the post from the public feed, sends a "Congratulations!" animation, and +1 is added to your database's "Total Successful Subleases" counter.

📊 3. Displaying DAU, MAU, and Match Data (Social Proof)
You are brilliant to want this on the app. Showing live data proves the app isn't a ghost town.

Don't use boring corporate terms like "DAU" or "MAU" on the user screen. Gamify it. Put a sleek, auto-updating "Live Campus Stats" banner right at the top of the Home Screen or Matchmaker screen.

Tell Replit to build a component that displays your database metrics like this:

🔥 142 Students Online Today (This is your DAU)

🤝 85 Roommate Groups Formed (This is your matches count)

🏠 23 Leases Signed This Week (Triggered by the "Mark as Leased" button)

This creates massive FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out). When a student opens the app and sees "23 Leases Signed This Week," they will immediately think, "Oh wow, people are actually getting apartments here, I need to swipe right now."
